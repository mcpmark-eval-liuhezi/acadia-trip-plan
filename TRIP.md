## Campground Recommendation: Blackwoods Campground

**Book: Blackwoods Campground — Tent Only Non-electric, $30/night — at Recreation.gov:**
https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232508

Why Blackwoods is the pick for a tent-only group that cares most about water and toilets:

1. **The best season overlap with our dates.** Blackwoods runs early May to mid-October — the longest season of Acadia's campgrounds. Seawall and Schoodic Woods both typically close around Columbus Day (Oct 12), which would already be behind us. **Call to confirm the exact closing date for 2026 when booking — mid-October is the norm, and our dates run right up against it.**
2. **Biggest tent inventory in the park.** 221 tent-only sites out of 281 total, versus 43 at Seawall and 13 at Schoodic Woods. Best odds of actually getting a site.
3. **Water + toilets, exactly what we care about.** Flush toilets (seasonal), potable water (seasonal), dump station, plus food storage lockers at sites. Heads-up: **no park campground in Acadia has showers** — none of the four.
4. **Location.** ~5 miles south of Bar Harbor on Route 3 — closest campground to town, the Park Loop Road, and the Hulls Cove Visitor Center. All sites wooded, all within a 10-minute walk of the ocean. Paved roads.
5. **Reservations required — no walk-ups.** All 281 sites are reservable-only via Recreation.gov; the campground office cannot book you. Release schedule: 90% of sites drop 6 months ahead (first of each month, 10 am EST); the remaining 10% drop 14 days before the arrival date on a rolling basis. For an Oct 23, 2026 arrival, that late batch released around Oct 9, 2026.

**Fall-back:** Seawall Campground (west side of the island, same water/toilet amenities, quieter, 43 tent sites) — https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/234674 — though its season also typically ends around Columbus Day.

| Reservations needed? | Yes — all sites via Recreation.gov | Yes — all sites via Recreation.gov | Yes — all sites via Recreation.gov | Yes — all sites; released Apr 1 for the season |

Notes:
- **No campground in the park has showers** — all four list "Showers (None)".
- **Duck Harbor is not practical for us:** remote, boat-only, 5 lean-to sites, max 3-night stay, 6 people per site, no pets, all trash carried out.
- Park entrance fee is **separate** from camping fees and required while staying in the campground: $35/vehicle for 7 days (plus $30 motorcycle / $20 per-person options; $6 Cadillac Mountain timed-entry reservation if we drive up).

## Current Alerts Worth Knowing (as of late October 2026)

All three are area closures — **none affect camping at Blackwoods**, but two touch hikes we'd plausibly do:

1. **Southern Section of Ocean Path CLOSED** — from the trailhead across from the Otter Point parking lot to the trail's southern terminus (in effect since Aug 31). If Ocean Path (Sand Beach → Otter Cliff) was on the list, that stretch is out.
2. **Eastern Section of Beech Mountain Loop Trail CLOSED** — from a junction just past the parking lot to just below the summit/fire tower (since Sept 21). Alternate summit routes: the outer loop of Beech Mountain Loop, Beech South Ridge Trail, or Beech West Ridge Trail.
3. **Schoodic Mountain Road CLOSED** — from Schoodic Loop Road to the summit of Schoodic Head (since Aug 31); heavy equipment doing resurfacing/regrading/ditching. Only matters if we day-trip to the Schoodic Peninsula.

Weather heads-up for late October: Acadia fall temps run 30–70°F and the first frost typically hits mid-October. Pack for cold nights.
